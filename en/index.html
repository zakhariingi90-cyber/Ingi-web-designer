FAQ

Got any questions?

Here are the answers to the questions I'm asked most often. Can't find yours? Get in touch.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what you need: how many pages, which features, and whether I also take care of copy and photos. Tell me about your project and I'll prepare a tailored quote, clear and with no surprises.

How long does it take?

It depends on the project: a landing page takes less time than an online store. Every quote includes the delivery time, so you know from the start when you'll be online.

What do I need to get started?

Just an idea of what you want to communicate. If you already have a logo, copy and photos, we'll use them; if not, I'll help you prepare them.

Will my website look good on a smartphone?

Yes: every project is designed for phones first, where most visits come from, and then adapted to tablets and computers.

Do you also handle social media?

Yes: I create and manage sponsored campaigns on Instagram and Facebook, to bring new people to your website and grow your business.

Do you work with clients outside Italy?

Yes: everything can be done remotely, by WhatsApp, email or video call, in Italian or English.
